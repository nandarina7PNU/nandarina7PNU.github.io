Hi, I'm Hyuna Park (박현아), a third-year undergraduate majoring in Artificial Intelligence at the College of Information and Biomedical Engineering, Pusan National University.

I'm interested in **video generative models** — how a model comes to represent motion, how temporal consistency holds up (or breaks down) across frames, and what actually makes generated video feel coherent rather than merely sharp. I'm currently working through the fundamentals behind them, and writing up what I learn as I go.

This site is where I keep those notes, along with my projects and CV. The blog is the part I update most often. Feel free to reach out.
